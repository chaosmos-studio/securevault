## Full description

Hide photos, videos and notes behind an app that looks ordinary. SecureVault turns into a working Calculator, Notes, Clock or MoodSpace app on your phone, and only your PIN opens the vault inside it.

It looks like a calculator
Pick a disguise when you set up. SecureVault takes its name and icon, and it really works: the calculator calculates, the clock keeps time, the notes save. To open your vault, type your PIN where nobody would look: on the calculator before =, in the notes search, on the timer, or as a mood note.

Set up in a minute
Choose a disguise and a PIN, then open your vault once on the disguise itself, so you know exactly how it works.

What you can keep
- Photos and videos, from your gallery or the camera
- Notes, with pins and colour tags
- Swipe through photos, zoom in, play videos, and share or save a copy when you need one

A second vault to show
Make another vault with its own PIN and a few harmless things in it. If someone makes you open SecureVault, type that PIN instead. It shows only its own photos and notes, and your main vault never appears in it.

Private by design
- Everything stays on your phone, encrypted with your PIN
- Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases
- No account and no tracking
- Screenshots are blocked while a vault is open
- It locks the moment you leave, or after a time you choose
- Back up a vault to one file and bring it back on a new phone

Good to know
SecureVault hides what you put inside it. It cannot hide other apps. Nobody can recover a forgotten PIN, so pick one you will remember and keep a backup.
